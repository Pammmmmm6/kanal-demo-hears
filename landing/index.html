What is Kanal?

Kanal is a WhatsApp marketing app for Shopify stores. It connects your store to the official WhatsApp Business API and gives you ready-made flows (cart recovery, order notifications, back in stock, reviews, upsell), a campaign builder, a shared inbox and an AI agent, all in one dashboard with revenue tracked per message.

How does Kanal connect to my Shopify store?

Install Kanal from the Shopify App Store. Your products, customers and orders sync automatically, so messages can show the right product, the right cart and the right order status. Most brands send their first flow in about five minutes.

Which flows can I automate?

Abandoned cart recovery, order confirmation, shipping and delivery updates, back-in-stock alerts, review collection, post-purchase upsell and VIP offers. The visual builder lets you add waits, conditions based on Shopify data, A/B tests and discount codes on the Scale plan and above.

How does the AI agent work?

The agent learns from your catalogue, your policies and your order data. It answers questions about sizing, stock, delivery and returns, recommends products and can track an order. When a question needs a person, it hands the conversation to your team in the Kanal inbox with the full context.

What kind of results can I expect?

It depends on your traffic and average order value. WhatsApp messages are read far more often than emails, which is why cart recovery is usually the first flow to pay for the subscription. Use the ROI calculator on getkanal.com with your own numbers to estimate the extra monthly revenue.

How is WhatsApp different from email?

Email lands in a crowded inbox and often in promotions or spam. WhatsApp lands next to messages from friends and family, gets read within minutes and lets customers reply. That turns a one-way blast into a conversation that can answer an objection and close a sale.

Which tools does Kanal integrate with?

Shopify at the core, plus Klaviyo, Gorgias, Recharge, Crisp, Loyoly, Zendesk and Splio, and a PrestaShop connector. A developer API is available for custom needs.

What is Kanal MCP?

Kanal MCP connects your Kanal account to AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Codex or Cursor. You can ask for your performance, build a segment or draft a campaign in plain language, and review everything in Kanal before it goes out.

Is there a commitment? Can you set it up for me?

There is no commitment on any plan: you pay monthly and can cancel anytime. If you'd rather not build the flows yourself, the Business plan includes setup and continuous optimisation by Kanal's experts, and the Agency plan adds strategy, creative and copywriting.
